01 Our mission

Hospital-level care, at human scale.

A Congregate Living Health Facility carries the clinical capability of a hospital subacute unit into a licensed residence of six beds. Ventilator weaning, tracheostomy care, complex wounds, IV therapy — delivered where every patient is known by name, by history, and by what matters to them.

Our mission is to build a network of CLHFs that earns the trust of hospitals and families alike: compassionate, high-acuity skilled nursing in modern, home-like environments — built new, staffed deep, and designed around the people inside them.

02 Why Calmora

What we're building differently

i.

New construction on every campus: natural daylight, quiet rooms, gardens outside real windows. Environment shapes outcomes — so we treat architecture as part of the care plan.

04 Clinical programs

The full continuum of subacute care

Respiratory & ventilator care

Ventilator management and weaning, tracheostomy care, and respiratory therapy — for patients who no longer need an ICU but still need someone watching every breath.

Complex skilled nursing

24-hour registered nursing for complex wounds, IV therapy, enteral nutrition, and medication management.

Rehabilitation therapies

Physical, occupational, and speech therapy woven into daily life, not scheduled around it. Progress is measured in what matters: a first step, a first word, a meal at the table.

Whole-person support

Nutritional services, social services, and discharge planning from the day of admission — because the goal of every stay is the life that comes after it.

Conditions we treat

  • Stroke
  • Brain injury
  • Spinal cord injury
  • Ventilator dependence
  • Neurological disorders
  • Post-surgical recovery
  • Complex medical conditions
  • Multiple trauma
